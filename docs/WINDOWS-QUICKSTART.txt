LP2 LightBurn Bridge - Windows quick start (scan-8)

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a writable folder, for example in Documents.
2. Double-click LP2Bridge.exe. Python is already bundled.
3. USB: power the LP2, attach USB, choose USB and its CH340 COM port.
   Bluetooth: keep external power connected, UNPLUG USB, choose Bluetooth,
   click Scan and select the LP2.
4. Click Connect and wait for Connected - Idle.
5. Create/select a generic GRBL device in LightBurn:
   Ethernet/TCP, 127.0.0.1, port 23230, 100 x 100 mm,
   front-left origin, Absolute Coordinates, auto-home OFF, S-value max 1000.
   Name it LP2 USB BRIDGE (the same profile works for Bluetooth).
6. Keep bridge rotation 180 and whole-job left/right correction enabled.
   Keep the LightBurn artwork unmirrored. Check a small asymmetric design.
7. Read README.md for the first-test workflow and operating limits.
   Guides are in docs/. No engraving starts automatically when the app opens.

Use normal LP2 enclosure/protection and material precautions. Supervise the job.
Stop job cancels it; interrupted bulk uploads may require restarting the LP2.
The app is experimental and is qualified only against the development LP2 unit.
Settings, profiles and private captures are stored in LP2BridgeData beside the EXE.
